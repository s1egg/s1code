# Panes

A thread's terminal, diff, browser, files, pull request, and other tools open as panes beside the
chat on web and desktop. You arrange them yourself: drag a pane next to the chat, under it, or
beside another pane, and the layout stays with the thread.

## Open and close panes

The icons at the right end of the chat's header open and hide the common panes: thread details,
**Terminal** (`mod+j`), **Diff** (`mod+d`), **Browser** (`mod+shift+j`), **Files**, and **Pull
request**. A pane that is showing has a gray background behind its icon. While a pane fills the
window, the icons move to that pane's header. The **…** menu next to them (`mod+t` while
a pane is open) opens **Side chat**, **Device**, **Linked items**, and an issue the thread has open,
and **Close all panes** closes everything.

The first pane opens to the right of the chat. Later ones open under the pane you last used, and a
pane you hid or closed comes back where it was.

Hiding a pane with its icon keeps what it shows: terminals keep running and a browser keeps its
page, which keeps playing in a small window over the chat. The **×** on a pane's name closes it
instead, which ends its terminals or browser tabs. `mod+shift+t` reopens what you closed last.
`mod+alt+b` hides every pane at once and brings the same layout back.

Each pane shows one thing at a time. When a pane holds more than one, such as several browser
pages, files, or side chats, the arrow next to its name switches between them. Pages an agent
opens without bringing them to the front wait there: the Browser pane's name shows how many, and
they are marked in its list. A speaker next to the name means the page is playing sound; mute it
from the pane's **…** menu.

## Minimize a pane

The minimize button next to a pane's **…** shrinks it into a small window in the bottom-right
corner of the chat, just above the composer. It keeps running there: terminals keep their output
and pages keep playing. Minimize several and they stack upward; when the chat is too short for all
of them, the rest wait behind a **+N** button at the top of the stack. The expand button on the small
window puts the pane back where it was, as does the pane's icon at the top, and the **×** closes it
just as closing the pane would. Press Escape inside a small window to go back to the composer.

A minimized browser or device shows its page or screen alone; drag it by the dot in its corner
and resize it from any edge. Minimized panes stay with the thread, and hide while a pane fills the
window.

## Arrange panes

Drag a pane by its name. While you drag, the half of the chat or pane under the pointer lights up
with **Move here**; let go to split that space and put the pane there. Press Escape to cancel. To
move a pane without dragging, open its **…** menu and choose **Move to left**, **right**, **top**,
or **bottom** of the chat.

Drag the line between two panes to resize them. Double-click it to go back to the default sizes.

The expand button on a pane fills the window with it. Choose **Show session** to bring the chat and
the other panes back.

Thread details stay docked at the right edge while no pane sits to the right of the chat. Otherwise
the thread details button opens them as a popover.

In a narrow window there is no room to tile, so panes open one at a time in a sheet with a row of
names to switch between them; minimized panes are in that row too, and picking one brings it back.
Closing the sheet keeps a browser or device it was showing playing over the chat. The layout comes
back when the window is wide again.
